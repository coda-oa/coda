About CODA

CODA (Customizable Open Access Database Application) is a database-based Open Access workflow tool developed at the University Library of Technische Universität Braunschweig as part of the project ADoRe-OA (Analysis and Development of Reusable OA-Applications).

The project was funded by the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space.
